# NutriCompass Master Plan

## Project Vision

To become the world's most trusted evidence-based nutrition platform, helping people make healthier food choices through simple, accurate, and scientifically backed information.

---

## Mission

NutriCompass empowers people with reliable nutrition knowledge by connecting foods, nutrients, symptoms, health conditions, and recipes in one easy-to-understand platform.

---

## Core Values

- Evidence-Based Information
- Simplicity
- Trust & Transparency
- User-First Design
- Continuous Improvement

---

## Target Audience

Primary Audience:
- General Public
- Parents
- Working Professionals
- Students
- Fitness Enthusiasts

Secondary Audience:
- Nutritionists
- Healthcare Professionals
- Researchers

---

## Product Goal (MVP)

The first version of NutriCompass will include:

- Home
- Symptoms
- Health Conditions
- Health Goals
- Foods
- Nutrients
- Recipes
- Articles
